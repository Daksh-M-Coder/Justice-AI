Right to Information Act, 2005

RTI Filing Guide

The RTI Act gives every Indian citizen the right to request information from any government office. Here's how to file one in 5 simple steps.

Step 01
Identify the Public Authority
Determine which government department or ministry holds the information.
Step 02
Draft Your Application
Write a specific request. Use our AI below to draft it instantly and formally.
Step 03
Pay the Fee
Application fee is ₹10 (BPL holders exempt). Pay via DD, postal order, or online.
Step 04
Submit Application
Online at rtionline.gov.in (Centre) or submit in person/post to the PIO.
Step 05
Wait for Response
The PIO responds within 30 days. If unsatisfied, file a First Appeal.
